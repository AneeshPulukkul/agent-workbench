# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (v2.1, abridged)

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Agent Operations Workbench a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes: demonstrating empathy, giving and gracefully
accepting constructive feedback, accepting responsibility and apologizing,
and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes: sexualized language or imagery, trolling or
insulting comments, public or private harassment, publishing others' private
information without permission, and any conduct inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may ban
contributors who violate this Code. Reports are handled privately via GitHub
Private vulnerability reporting or by contacting the maintainers listed in
`CODEOWNERS`. All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially.

Violations may result in: (1) correction, (2) warning, (3) temporary ban,
(4) permanent ban, per the full Covenant enforcement ladder.
